# Getting Started

The map is the first layer of the Abyss Guide. Data generation writes its nodes, article metadata, and explicit connections to JSON. Markdown provides the article bodies.

Click an item node to expand that node into a centered card. The card keeps the selected node visible while it shows the node summary and its article list.

## Reading the guide

- Hover an item with guide content and hold R until the tooltip progress bar fills to Recall its related node. Releasing R lets the progress recede. The tooltip shows the rebound key if you customize it; closing the guide returns to the previous screen.

- Drag the empty map to pan the view. Double-click blank space to close the open card; click another node to retract the current card and open the new one. Moving the card into a screen edge also closes it.
- Use the mouse wheel to zoom the entire view around the cursor, including labels and open cards.
- Drag with the middle mouse button to pan while a card is open.
- Click an article row to expand it inline; switching rows smoothly collapses the old article and opens the new one.
- Scroll inside the card when an article is longer than the available space.
- Drag inside a 3D scene to rotate it, scroll to zoom it, and double-click to reset its camera.
- Use the scene's upper-right buttons for playback and animated fullscreen. Click the bottom progress bar to jump to a keyframe, the beginning, or the end.
- Press Esc to collapse the article, close the card, and return to the map.

Read [Star Compass](guide:star_compass) for an interactive ruins scene. Articles support **bold text**, *emphasis*, `inline code`, lists, quotes, code blocks, and links.
